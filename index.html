ABOUT THIS PROJECT

From a book to a living encyclopedia.

Hemplopedia began as a published reference book by Willis Butts. This online edition preserves its topic directory and provides direct paths to articles, images, and videos about each subject.

Research is an ongoing process. These initial topic pages are indexes, not fully researched articles. The book’s generic descriptions have not been presented as verified facts. Future editions will add independently sourced explanations and deeper references.
